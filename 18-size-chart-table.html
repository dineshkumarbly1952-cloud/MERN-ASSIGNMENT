Measurement Tips

  • Chest / Bust: Measure around the fullest part of the chest, keeping the tape horizontal.
  • Waist: Measure around the narrowest part of the waistline (typically just above the belly button).
  • Hips: Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of the hip/seat area.
  • If your measurements fall between two sizes, we recommend ordering the larger size for a more comfortable fit.
