CSS Text Overflow Techniques

1. Single-Line Truncation (Ellipsis)

Yeh ek bahut hi lamba sentence hai jo ek hi line mein rahega aur aage jaakar teen dots (...) mein convert ho jayega.

2. Multi-Line Clamp (2 Lines)

Yeh multi-line text overflow ko handle karne ke liye use hota hai. Isme text sirf do lines tak dikhega aur uske baad automatically cut ho jayega bina layout kharab kiye.
