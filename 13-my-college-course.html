Environmental Engineering

Engineering Sustainable Solutions for a Greener Tomorrow

B.Tech Environmental Engineering is an interdisciplinary undergraduate program combining principles of engineering, chemistry, biology, and earth sciences to protect public health and preserve natural ecosystems.

Program Overview

The curriculum focuses on developing advanced technologies and management practices for water purification, air pollution control, solid and hazardous waste management, renewable energy integration, and environmental impact assessments.

Students gain practical laboratory experience, fieldwork exposure, and analytical skills necessary to tackle pressing global challenges such as climate change, resource depletion, and pollution mitigation.

Core Subjects & Focus Areas

Water & Wastewater Engineering

Study of municipal water supply systems, sewage treatment plants, hydraulic design, and advanced purification processes.
